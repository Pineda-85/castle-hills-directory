Castle Hills Business Alliance ("we", "us") runs the Castle Hills Business Alliance Directory at [YOUR-DOMAIN.com] (the "Site"). This policy explains what information the Site shows and what it collects.

Business information we publish

The directory lists businesses in and around Castle Hills, Texas. For each listing we may show the business name, category, address, phone number, website, email address, and the name of a contact person. This information comes from a business list kept by the Castle Hills Business Association (September 2021), and from information businesses give us. It is business contact information. We do not publish home addresses, and we do not collect payment information.

What we collect from visitors

  • We do not use accounts, ads, analytics or tracking tools. The Site does not set cookies.
  • Light/dark setting. If you choose light or dark mode, your choice is saved in your own browser (local storage) so the Site remembers it. It is not sent to us.
  • Search terms. Searching and filtering happen in your browser. We do not receive what you type.
  • Server logs. Our web host, HostGator, automatically records technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and operation. We do not use these logs to identify individual visitors.

Links to other sites

Listings link to business websites and third-party pages. The "Get directions" buttons open Google Maps, Apple Maps or Waze in a new tab, and only when you click them. We do not control those sites or services and are not responsible for their privacy practices.
